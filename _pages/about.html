I am an undergraduate in statistics at Nanjing University, working on optimization for deep learning: how momentum, EMA-style lookahead and the geometry of matrix updates interact with the noise in stochastic gradients.

My research interests are machine learning, large language models, reinforcement learning and optimization. I try to keep the notes, slides and code behind each project together, so that the work can be reproduced rather than just described.
